What the App Does

The app lets your customers build their own bundle from products you choose. Once they select the required number of products, they automatically receive your offer.

Before adding a product to the bundle, customers can choose its variant, such as size and color. This means one bundle can include different sizes and colors of the same or different products.

TipBundles work best when the products are related, for example t-shirts, socks or skincare items.

Installation

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Open the app from your Shopify Admin.
  3. Create or configure a bundle offer inside the app.
  4. Go to Online Store > Themes > Customize.
  5. Add the “Bundle Template” app block to the page or section where you want it.
  6. Save and preview your storefront.

Bundle Discounts: step-by-step setup

Use this guide when you want Shopify to apply an automatic discount after a customer adds a qualifying bundle to their cart. You do not need technical knowledge—work through the sections in order and use the example values as a starting point.

Before you startDecide what you want shoppers to see. For example: “Buy any 3 items for ₹1,999.” You will use this wording for the offer name and cart message.
1. Offer details 2. Limits & combinations 3. Eligibility & schedule

1. Name the offer and choose how shoppers save

Give the offer a clear internal name, such as BUY 3 AT 1999. Add a short customer message, choose when it starts, and leave the end date empty if you want the offer to keep running.

Under Choose how shoppers save, select the discount type that matches your promotion:

  • Fixed bundle price: set one total price for the whole bundle. Example: 3 items for ₹1,999.
  • Percentage off: take a percentage off the qualifying items. Example: buy 3 and get 15% off.
  • Buy X Get Y Free: give an item free when shoppers buy the required quantity. Example: buy 2, get 1 free.

For a fixed-price offer, enter the bundle quantity and the bundle price. Use Add tier only when you want more than one offer level, such as 3 for ₹1,999 and 5 for ₹2,999. The optional tier cap limits how often that tier can be used.

2. Decide how often the discount can apply

Choose the rule that feels right for your promotion:

  • Apply once per order: one qualifying bundle receives the discount. This is the simplest choice for most offers.
  • Repeat for every qualifying bundle: the discount repeats for every complete group in the cart.
  • Limit number of bundles: set a maximum number of complete bundles that can receive the discount.
  • Limit discounted items: set a maximum number of eligible items that can be discounted.

Keep Cheapest eligible items first if you want the discount to apply to the lowest-priced qualifying items. Under Discount combinations, tick Product, Order, or Shipping only when you want this offer to stack with that type of Shopify discount.

3. Choose eligible products, customers, and timing

Use Product eligibility to choose whether the offer works on all products or only specific collections. For an offer such as “Buy 3 at 1999,” select the collection containing the products shoppers may mix and match. Use Choose collections to add it.

Leave Customer eligibility as All customers to make the promotion available to everyone. Only use customer segments, excluded product IDs, or minimum values when you have a specific advanced rule to apply. You can safely leave those fields blank for a standard offer.

Finally, set the start date and time, add an end date only for a limited campaign, and enter the Cart message shoppers should see after the discount is applied.

Final checkSave the offer, then add the required products to your storefront cart and confirm that the message and discount appear as expected.

Checkout Settings

Choose which checkout your customers use after completing a bundle:

  • Shopify Checkout – the standard Shopify checkout.
  • Shiprocket Checkout
  • GoKwik Checkout
  • Custom Checkout
NoteShiprocket and GoKwik must already be installed and enabled in your published Shopify theme before you select them here.

Custom Checkout

Custom Checkout gives you two options:

  • Custom button HTML – the HTML of the checkout button shown to customers.
  • Custom checkout JavaScript (optional) – code that runs when the customer clicks the button.
<button type="button" class="my-checkout-btn">Buy Now</button>
WarningNever include private API keys or secrets in storefront code. Anyone visiting your store can see it.

Pin a Product to the Top

You can place a specific product at the top of the bundle page by adding its product handle to the page URL.

Format

https://your-store.com/pages/your-bundle-page?attop=product-handle

Example

https://warriorworld.in/pages/home-page?attop=trekkers-stylish-soft-cotton-t-shirt
ImportantThe query parameter key is exactly attop. The value must be the Shopify product handle.

To find a product handle, open the product in Shopify Admin and look at the URL handle in the Search engine listing section.

Troubleshooting

The Bundle Template does not appear on the page

Make sure the block is added in the theme editor on the correct page, and that you clicked Save. Check that the theme you edited is the published one.

Products are not showing in the bundle

Check that a Products collection is selected, the collection has products, and the products are active and available on your Online Store sales channel.

The pinned product does not appear first

Confirm the URL uses exactly ?attop= and that the value is the correct product handle. The product must also be in the selected collection.

Checkout button is not working

Check your checkout selection. For Custom Checkout, review your button HTML and JavaScript for mistakes.

GoKwik or Shiprocket button is unavailable

The app must be installed and enabled in your published theme. Check that it works on your normal cart page first.

Discount is not applied as expected

Check the Discount type, Required quantity and Discount value. Make sure the customer has selected the full required quantity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can customers choose size and color?

Yes. Customers choose product variants before adding items to their bundle.

Can I offer more than one type of discount?

Each Bundle Template block uses one discount type. You can add different blocks on different pages for different offers.

Can customers add more items after the offer is complete?

Yes, if you turn on Allow additional products.

Which checkouts are supported?

Shopify Checkout, Shiprocket Checkout, GoKwik Checkout and Custom Checkout.

How do I show a specific product first?

Add ?attop=product-handle to your bundle page URL.

Is it safe to add API keys in custom checkout code?

No. Never put private API keys in storefront code.
